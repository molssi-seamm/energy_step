energy_step.energy module¶

Non-graphical part of the Energy step in a SEAMM flowchart.

The Energy step evaluates the energy, and optionally the gradients (forces) and stress, of one or many structures using the Model Chemistry published upstream (the _model_chemistry workspace variable), driving the program as a resident MDI engine via seamm_mdi. The engine is started once per distinct topology (elements, charge, multiplicity, periodicity) and then fed the structures one after another over the warm connection, so labelling hundreds of structures – e.g. with a machine-learned force field – costs a single engine start-up.

Results are stored per configuration: the energy, gradients and stress as properties, and the gradients also on the atoms (so downstream writers such as the extended-XYZ format emit them as forces).

energy_step.metadata module¶

This file contains metadata describing the results from the Energy step.

energy_step.metadata.metadata = {'results': {'configuration name': {'description': 'The name of the configuration evaluated', 'dimensionality': 'scalar', 'type': 'string'}, 'elapsed time': {'description': 'Wall-clock time for this evaluation', 'dimensionality': 'scalar', 'format': '.3f', 'type': 'float', 'units': 's'}, 'energy': {'description': 'The energy of the structure', 'dimensionality': 'scalar', 'format': '.3f', 'property': 'energy#Energy#{model}', 'type': 'float', 'units': 'kJ/mol'}, 'gradients': {'description': 'The gradients on the atoms', 'dimensionality': '[n_atoms][3]', 'format': '.3f', 'property': 'gradients#Energy#{model}', 'type': 'json', 'units': 'kJ/mol/Å'}, 'maximum force': {'description': 'The largest force component on any atom', 'dimensionality': 'scalar', 'format': '.3f', 'type': 'float', 'units': 'kJ/mol/Å'}, 'model chemistry': {'description': 'The model chemistry (level spec) used', 'dimensionality': 'scalar', 'type': 'string'}, 'rms force': {'description': 'The root-mean-square force component', 'dimensionality': 'scalar', 'format': '.3f', 'type': 'float', 'units': 'kJ/mol/Å'}, 'stress': {'description': 'The stress tensor (periodic systems only)', 'dimensionality': '[3][3]', 'format': '.4f', 'property': 'stress#Energy#{model}', 'type': 'json', 'units': 'GPa'}}}¶

Results that the Energy step produces.

The step carries no computational model of its own – the energy comes from the Model Chemistry published upstream, driven over MDI – so only results is populated. {model} in the property names is filled from the model chemistry’s level spec at run time (e.g. xnn:MLFF@water or MOPAC:SQM@PM6-ORG).

The energy, gradients and stress are always stored as properties on every configuration evaluated (that is the point of the step); the entries here additionally let the user save them, and the summary values, to variables or tables. store_results is called once per configuration, so a table gets one row per structure.
